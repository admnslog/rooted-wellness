PRF Under-Eye Rejuvenation

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF)

  • Per treatment$215

Platelet-Rich Fibrin (PRF) is an advanced regenerative treatment created from your own blood. Often considered the next generation of PRP, PRF contains a higher concentration of platelets, white blood cells, and a natural fibrin matrix. The fibrin matrix allows growth factors to be released gradually over time, helping support collagen production and natural tissue rejuvenation. PRF is commonly used to improve the appearance of the under-eye area by supporting healthier-looking skin.
