Concept Of IV Therapy

Intravenous therapy is one of the most efficient ways to deliver essential nutrients directly into the bloodstream. Unlike oral supplements, IV support bypasses digestive limitations and allows immediate access at the cellular level.

At Rooted Wellness, we use IV infusions to support hydration, energy, recovery, immune function, detoxification, and nutrient replenishment. Each plan is selected with your symptoms, goals, and broader clinical picture in mind.
