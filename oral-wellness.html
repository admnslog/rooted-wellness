Orforglipron (Oral GLP-1)

$150

Orforglipron is an investigational oral GLP-1 receptor agonist designed to support appetite regulation, blood sugar management, weight management, and metabolic health.

Designed to support:

  • checkAppetite regulation
  • checkBlood sugar management
  • checkWeight management
  • checkMetabolic health

Unlike injectable GLP-1 medications, Orforglipron is a non-peptide, once-daily oral medication. Clinical studies have demonstrated meaningful weight loss and improvements in several cardiometabolic risk factors, including blood sugar, blood pressure, and LDL cholesterol. Availability may depend on regulatory approval and prescribing guidelines.
