Why Injectables?

When you take a vitamin orally, it passes through your digestive system — where absorption can be limited by gut health, food interactions, and individual variation. Injectable nutrients skip that process entirely, entering your bloodstream directly for near-immediate availability at the cellular level.

This makes injectables particularly effective for people with absorption challenges, known deficiencies, or those who want to maximize the impact of a targeted nutrient protocol. Every injectable at Rooted Wellness is administered by our clinical team and chosen in the context of your labs and goals.
